    Descriptions

    Short

    Draft is a free notes app for the Mac that keeps your words as plain files you own, with styles that make every page your own and a private assistant that runs on your Mac.

    Long

    Draft is a free, local-first notes and document app for macOS. There's no account and no telemetry: notes are files in a documented format on your Mac. It has blocks, tables, collections, tasks, daily notes and links; original styles and backgrounds; Markdown, PDF, image and website export; a local AI Assistant that works offline; and an MCP server through which AI agents such as Codex and Claude Code propose changes that you review before anything happens.
