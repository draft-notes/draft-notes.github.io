    First launch

    Opening Draft the first time

    This build is signed with its developer’s Apple Development certificate, but not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once. It takes a minute, and only the first time.

    1. .zip→→Applications

      Unzip and move to Applications

      Open the downloaded .zip (Safari may already have unzipped it) and drag Draft into the Applications folder.

    2. Move to BinDone

      Open it, then click Done

      macOS says it couldn't verify Draft. That's expected for apps that aren't notarized. Click Done, not Move to Bin.

    3. “Draft” was blocked… Open Anyway

      Open Anyway in Privacy & Security

      Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Draft and click Open Anyway.

    4. CancelOpen

      Confirm, and you're in

      Confirm with your password or Touch ID. From now on Draft opens like any other app.

    Why isn't it notarized?

    Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer ID, which Draft doesn't have yet. Until then each build is signed by its developer with the hardened runtime, and you can check it yourself: the checksum above, and codesign --verify --deep --strict /Applications/Draft.app in Terminal, which prints nothing when the signature is intact. On macOS 14 you can also Control-click Draft and choose Open; macOS 15 and later use the Privacy & Security step above. More in the install guide.
