    03 / GET SET UP

    From download
    to first thought.

    This build is signed with its developer’s Apple Development certificate, but not notarized by Apple yet. macOS asks for your approval once on first launch.

    1. 01

      Open the disk image

      Double-click the downloaded DMG. Drag Draft into the Applications shortcut, then eject the Draft disk image.

    2. 02

      Open Draft

      If macOS says it cannot verify Draft, click Done to close the notice.

    3. 03

      Choose Open Anyway

      In System Settings → Privacy & Security, find Draft and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

    4. 04

      Make it yours

      Draft opens normally from then on. Your notes stay as files on this Mac.
