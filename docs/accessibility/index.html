    Accessibility

    VoiceOver, keyboard use, Reduce Motion and contrast in Draft.

    Keyboard

    Everything in Draft has a keyboard path: menus list every command with its shortcut, the slash menu inserts any block, and Esc selects blocks so you can move them with the arrow keys.

    VoiceOver

    Controls have labels and states (a bold selection reads as “Bold, on”). Images use their alt text as their VoiceOver label; set it from the image bar. On a board, cards offer Move to actions instead of dragging.

    Motion

    Draft follows Reduce Motion in System Settings, and has its own Reduce animations setting. Moving effects such as Spectrum stay still.

    Contrast

    Every built-in style has at least 4.5:1 contrast between text and page. If your own colours fall below that, the Style panel warns you and offers to fix the text colour. Draft follows Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency, replacing glass with solid surfaces.
