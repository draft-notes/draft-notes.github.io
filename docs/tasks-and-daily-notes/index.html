    Writing

    Tasks, daily notes and calendar

    Checklists anywhere, one Tasks view for all of them, and a note for every day.

    Tasks

    Start a line with [] or x and a space, or press ⇧⌘9, to make a checklist item. Add a due date with @ and a date. Tasks stay in the note they belong to.

    The Tasks view

    Tasks (⇧⌥⌘2) gathers tasks from every note: Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Completed, with counts. Ticking a task here ticks it in its note too, and one Undo reverts it. The Tasks tab of the document rail shows just the tasks in the current note.

    Choose how completed tasks look (struck through, dimmed or unchanged) in Settings → Daily Notes & Tasks.

    Daily notes

    ⇧⌘D opens today’s note, creating it from your daily template the first time. Previous and next day are in the top bar, or ⇧⌥⌘← and ⇧⌥⌘→.

    If you work late, set when your day starts in Settings → Advanced: before that hour, “today” is still the previous day.

    Calendar

    Calendar (⇧⌥⌘1) shows your days with their notes and tasks. Add a task to a future day and it’s due that day.
