    Privacy

    Nothing leaves your Mac unless you turn it on.

    Short version: Draft has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Your notes are files on your Mac. This website doesn't track you either.

    In the app

    • Your notes, images and files stay in a folder on your Mac. Draft never uploads them.
    • There's no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting and no “phoning home”.
    • Help → Report a Problem writes a local text file you review first; note text and titles are left out, and nothing is sent.
    • Settings → Privacy lists every permission and network feature, with a button to each setting.
    • The Assistant runs on your Mac and works with networking off. Dictation uses Apple’s on-device recognizer only.
    • AI agents connected over MCP run on your Mac, and every change they propose waits for your review. Draft logs which tool ran on which note and when, never the note’s text.

    Things that use the network, only when you turn them on

    FeatureBy default
    Update checksOnly when you click Check for Updates; automatic checks are off until you turn them on. A plain HTTPS request for the update list and, if you choose to download, the archive. Nothing about you.
    Image search (Openverse)Off. Asks before the first search.
    Link previewsOff. A pasted link stays text until you turn previews on.
    Downloading the Assistant’s modelOnly when you click Download. Checked against its published SHA-256.
    OpenAI provider for the AssistantOff. Uses your own key; shows exactly what will be sent and waits for your OK every time.

    On this website

    • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything is served from this site.
    • The home page remembers, for this browser tab only (session storage), that its opening animation already played. Nothing else is stored.
    • Search runs entirely in your browser on an index stored on this site.
    • Our host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security; we don’t use them to track visitors.
