    Features

    Everything in Draft, on one page.

    Writing, structure, styles, search, tasks, a private Assistant and agents that ask first, in one calm Mac app.

    WriteStructureStyleFindTasks & CalendarAssistantAgentsExport & share

    Write

    A calm page that formats as you go.

    • Markdown shortcuts turn into headings, lists, quotes and code as you type.
    • Select words for the glass formatting bar: bold, links, highlights and text effects.
    • Accent, Gradient, Glow and Spectrum effects, with a gradient editor for your own colours.

    Structure

    Blocks, tables and collections in one note.

    • Drag any block to reorder it, or press Alt with ↑ or ↓.
    • Tables you edit in place, and collections you can show as a table, gallery or board.
    • Pages nest inside pages, so a project keeps everything together.

    Style

    Every note can have its own mood.

    • Built-in styles set the paper, fonts, accent and background in one click.
    • Twenty-four backgrounds drawn by Draft itself: gradients, dots, patterns and scenes.
    • Block effects frame a paragraph or callout without changing your words.

    Find

    Everything is a few letters away.

    • Quick Open finds notes and blocks as you type.
    • Links, backlinks and tags connect ideas across your Space.
    • Recently Deleted keeps removed notes until you empty it.

    Tasks & Calendar

    Your days in the same place as your notes.

    • Tasks live next to the words they belong to, with due dates.
    • Daily notes and a calendar view bring each day together.

    Assistant

    An assistant that stays on your Mac.

    • Ask about the note you are in, a selection or your whole Space.
    • Answers show the sources they used; edits arrive as a preview you accept or discard.
    • Local by default and offline; online models only with your own key, after you see what is sent.

    Agents

    AI agents that ask first.

    • Codex, Claude Code and other MCP clients can read notes through Draft’s MCP server, once you allow it.
    • Every change they propose waits for your review, with before and after.
    • Apply, reject or leave it for later; one Undo reverses an applied change.

    Export & share

    Take your notes anywhere.

    • Export as Markdown, a styled web page, PDF or an image.
    • Publish a whole library as a small static website.
    • Notes are files in a documented format you can read without Draft.

    Try it with your own notes.
