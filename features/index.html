    THE DRAFT EXPERIENCE

    One place.
    Every kind of idea.

    Find the right shape for whatever is on your mind.

    Write↗Structure↗Style↗Find↗Days↗Assistant↗Agents↗Export↗

    01 / Write

    Follow the thought.

    Begin with a sentence. Let headings, callouts, tables, links and text effects appear when you want them. The page is yours to shape.

    • Rich text and Markdown shortcuts
    • Formatting that stays out of your way
    • Original text and block effects

    02 / Structure

    Make the messy make sense.

    Move blocks around, nest a page inside another, or turn a pile of ideas into a collection. The structure follows the work.

    • Pages inside pages
    • Tables, galleries and boards
    • One-step undo for changes

    03 / Style

    Make it feel like yours.

    A style changes the entire atmosphere of a note. Pick an original background, fine-tune a gradient or choose an effect for a few words.

    • 13 built-in styles
    • 24 original backgrounds
    • Custom gradients and effects

    04 / Find

    Nothing gets lost.

    Search words inside notes, follow a link between ideas, or jump into a block directly. Tags and backlinks help you see the bigger picture.

    • Quick Open for notes and blocks
    • Links, tags and backlinks
    • Recently Deleted

    05 / Days

    Give today a page.

    Daily notes, tasks and a calendar keep plans next to the writing they belong to.

    • Daily notes and templates
    • Tasks across documents
    • A calendar for your work

    06 / Assistant

    Think with your notes.

    Ask a question across your Space or get help with a paragraph. Draft shows its sources and lets you review every edit.

    • A local model runs offline
    • Answers cite your notes
    • You choose which edits to apply

    07 / Agents

    Help that asks first.

    Codex, Claude Code and other MCP agents can work with your notes through Draft. You decide what each agent may read and propose.

    • Permission controls per agent
    • Before-and-after review
    • Undo accepted changes

    08 / Export

    Take it with you.

    Your notes live as files on your Mac. Export a page or a whole site, and keep your ideas readable wherever you go.

    • Markdown, HTML, PDF and images
    • Offline site export
    • Documented file format
